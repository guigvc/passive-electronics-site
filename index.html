About Us

Built by tier-1 engineers

Passive Electronics Co. was established in Hong Kong in 2015 by a team with Apple, Huawei and other tier-1 product design and supply chain experience. Our manufacturing base in Shenzhen has generated an average annual revenue of USD 100 million over the past five years.

Who We Are

A Hong Kong-based company focused exclusively on custom IoT device solutions and commercial display systems, with in-house electronic component sourcing and supply chain support.

Full-Cycle Service

From Design → Proto → EVT → DVT → PVT → Mass Production, we cover the whole development cycle. Solutions can be delivered as CBU, SKD or CKD per your needs.

Scale & Trust

Sub-companies in Shenzhen, Shanghai, Beijing and Hong Kong with ~100 employees, supplying brands such as Huawei, Xiaomi, TCL, Hikvision, Dahua, ZTE and BYD.
